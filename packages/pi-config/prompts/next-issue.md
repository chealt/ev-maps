description: Pick up the next open issue by priority and start on it
---

Pick up the next issue, as CONTRIBUTING.md's "Picking Up Issues" says:
by its Priority field (Urgent, High, Medium, Low, then none), oldest
first, skipping issues that already have an open pull request. Skip a
parent with open sub-issues and take its sub-issues instead: one without
a Priority of its own has its parent's.

Say which issue and why in one line, read it, and start. If its direction
is unclear (a redesign, a product choice), propose one in a few bullets
and wait for an answer instead.
