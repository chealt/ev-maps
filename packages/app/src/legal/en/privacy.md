EV Maps (ev.chealt.com, "we") is a map of electric vehicle charging stations.
This policy explains which personal data we process, why, for how long, and what
your rights are.

## Who we are

EV Maps is operated by chealt, who is responsible for your personal data (the
controller). Contact: [info@chealt.com](mailto:info@chealt.com).

## What we process

### When you use the map

- **Technical data.** Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes your IP
  address and the details of each request (such as the page, browser and time)
  to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.
- **Errors.** When something goes wrong in the site or on our server, we store
  the error with the page, your browser and approximate country, and your
  account identifier if you're signed in, to fix it. We don't store your IP
  address with it.
- **Language and country.** When you open ev.chealt.com, we use your browser's
  language and your approximate country, derived from your IP address, to send
  you to the right page. We don't store them.
- **Map, place search and routes.** The map, place suggestions and route
  planning are provided by Google Maps Platform. Your browser sends these
  requests (such as the visible map area, the places you search for and the
  route you plan) directly to Google.
- **Chargers along a route.** When you plan a route, its line is sent to our
  server to find the chargers along it. We don't store it.
- **Your location.** Only if you allow it in your browser. It is used in your
  browser to centre the map and, if you choose, as the start of a route.
- **Settings in your browser.** Your theme, map position and filters are stored
  in your browser (local storage and IndexedDB), your chosen language in a
  cookie, and pages and charger data are cached by a service worker so the site
  works offline. They stay on your device; you can clear them in your browser at
  any time.

### When you sign in

When you sign in with Google, GitHub, Microsoft or Apple, we receive your name,
email address, profile picture and account identifier from that provider. We
store them in our database with your sign-in sessions, including each session's
IP address and browser. A session cookie keeps you signed in.

When you sign in with your email address, we store the address and send you a
sign-in link through our email provider; opening the link confirms the address
and creates your account the first time. If you add a passkey, we store its
public key, identifier and type (never your fingerprint, face or screen lock,
which stay on your device).

## Why we process it

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **To provide the service you ask for,** including your account (Article
  6(1)(b), contract).
- **To keep the service secure and prevent abuse,** for example with logs and
  rate limits (Article 6(1)(f), legitimate interests).
- **Your location,** only with your consent, which you give and withdraw in your
  browser (Article 6(1)(a)).

We don't sell personal data, use it for advertising, or profile you.

## Cookies and similar technologies

We use only what the site needs to work: a session cookie while you're signed
in, a short-lived cookie during sign-in, and a cookie for the language you
chose. We use no advertising or analytics cookies.

## Who else processes your data

- **Cloudflare, Inc.** hosts the site and its database and protects it, on our
  behalf.
- **Google LLC** provides the map, place search, route planning and Google
  sign-in, under the [Google Privacy
  Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **GitHub, Microsoft and Apple** process your data when you sign in with them,
  under their own privacy policies.
- **Resend, Inc.** sends the sign-in emails, on our behalf.

Some of these companies are in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU–U.S.
Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual
clauses.

## How long we keep it

- **Account data:** until you ask us to delete your account.
- **Sign-in sessions:** until they expire (7 days without use) or you sign out.
- **Server logs:** a few days at Cloudflare.
- **Errors:** 30 days.

## Your rights

You can ask us for access to your personal data, and to correct, delete,
restrict or export it, and you can object to its processing. Where we rely on
your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. To use these rights, or to have
your account deleted, email [info@chealt.com](mailto:info@chealt.com) from the
email address of your account. You can also complain to the data protection
authority in your country.

## Children

EV Maps isn't meant for children under 16, and we don't knowingly process their
data.

## Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
